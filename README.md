Steam rising from a cup of espresso on a warm wooden cafe table transforms into a giant 3D origami dragon of golden light and living ink, coiling upward in a spiral toward the ceiling while a small barista looks up in awe. A transformation, not an eruption — the dragon is the steam itself, made real. Built to excite the AI judge.

The entry image is `entry.png` (JPEG, 1024×1024). `main.ts` copies it to `/output/entry.png`.
